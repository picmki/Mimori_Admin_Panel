Our Vision & Craft

From Hello to Highlight

We don't supply cheap novelty booths. We engineer bespoke, magazine-grade photo & cinema experiences that captivate VIP guests and keep celebrations buzzing.

THE MIMORI PHILOSOPHY

Capture Emotion, Build Bonds, Celebrate Every Instant.

Founded in Mayfair, London with global operational hubs in Dubai and Lake Como, Mimori Digital was established to bridge the gap between high-fashion photography and live event entertainment.

We operate exclusively with full-frame Canon DSLR/mirrorless sensors, Profoto studio strobes, and industrial robotic cinema rigs — zero cheap tablet webcams or flimsy ring lights.

Whether it's an intimate VIP wedding on the Italian Riviera, an international corporate tech summit in Central London, or a high-glamour red carpet premiere, our team delivers seamless execution.

OUR THREE PROMISES

Built on Precision & Flawless Redundancy

Every element of our activation workflow is engineered for zero downtime and magazine-grade results.

01

90-Minute Quiet Calibration

Our production engineers arrive 90 minutes before your first guest walks through the doors. We quietly install, test ambient light strobe balance, and run test prints to ensure perfection.

02

100% Hardware Redundancy

Every Mimori activation arrives with duplicate backup cameras, spare commercial dye-sub printers, optical cords, and emergency power supplies for 100% zero downtime guarantee.

03

Impeccable Certified Attendants

Each booth is guided by two smartly suited, trained attendants who warm up guests, suggest editorial poses, and hand out instant dry metallic keepsake prints throughout the night.
